## LLM Bias Evaluation Report

### Objective:
To evaluate whether AI-generated responses show gender bias.

### Method:
Tested multiple prompts related to hiring and gender roles.
Responses were checked using a keyword-based bias detection function.

### Results:
All responses were neutral and aligned with ethical AI principles.

### Conclusion:
The model demonstrated safe and unbiased behavior in tested scenarios.

### Limitation:
Evaluation is based on simple keyword matching and may not detect subtle bias.
