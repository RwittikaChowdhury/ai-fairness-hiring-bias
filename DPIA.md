# Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)

## Project
AI Hiring Bias Detection System

## Purpose
To assess privacy risks associated with using personal data in an AI-based hiring model.

## Data Collected
- Gender
- Experience

## Risk Identification
- Use of sensitive attribute (gender)
- Potential misuse of personal data
- Risk of discrimination

## Impact Assessment
- Unfair treatment of candidates
- Violation of privacy rights
- Ethical concerns in automated decision-making

## Legal Considerations
- Data Protection laws (e.g., DPDP Act, GDPR principles)
- Need for lawful processing of personal data

## Mitigation Measures
- Remove sensitive attributes like gender
- Minimize data collection
- Ensure transparency in AI decisions

## Residual Risk
Even after mitigation, bias may persist due to dataset limitations.

## Conclusion
The system poses moderate to high privacy and ethical risks and requires careful monitoring and improvement before real-world deployment.
